Getting started

Do I need a piano?

No. You can play on the on-screen keys. For the best experience, connect a digital piano over Bluetooth or USB MIDI, or play an acoustic piano and let the App listen through the microphone.

The App does not hear my piano

Make sure microphone access is on in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Place the device close to the piano and play in a quiet room. Sound is analyzed on your device and never recorded.

My digital piano does not connect

For USB, use an Apple camera adapter or a USB-C cable and make sure the piano is on before opening the App. For Bluetooth MIDI, connect from the App’s piano setup screen, not from iOS Bluetooth settings.

Subscription

What is free?

Ode to Joy, the first three lessons, the first part of every song, and up to 15 minutes of playing a day. Full access opens every song, level, lesson and practice tool.

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get 3 days free. After the trial, the weekly subscription renews automatically until you cancel. You will not be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad, choose Learn to Play Piano: Practice and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Full access until the end of the current period.

I bought Full access but it is locked

Open the App, go to Settings and tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
