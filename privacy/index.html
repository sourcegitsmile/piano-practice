Privacy Policy

Learn to Play Piano: Practice · Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Learn to Play Piano: Practice app (the “App”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The App is published by Fire Technologies Hong Kong Limited (“we”, “us”).

In short: you do not need an account. Your songs, progress and practice stay on your device. We use Google Firebase to understand how the App is used, to fix crashes and, only if you allow it, to measure which ads bring people to the App.

1. Information that stays on your device

  • Practice data: your progress, favorites, saved loops, streak, goals and settings are stored only on your device.
  • MIDI files you import stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Microphone: if you choose to play an acoustic piano, the App listens through the microphone to check the notes you play. The sound is analyzed on your device in real time. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth and USB MIDI: if you connect a digital piano, the App reads the notes you play. This data stays on your device.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not use push notifications.

Deleting the App removes all of this data.

2. Information collected through Google Firebase

The App uses two Google services: Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) and Firebase Crashlytics. This information is not linked to your identity: we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or any account.

3. Tracking and App Tracking Transparency

The first time you open the App, iOS asks whether the App may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

  • If you choose Allow, Firebase Analytics may read your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to help us measure which advertising campaigns bring people to the App.
  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not accessed. The App works exactly the same.

You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. We do not show ads in the App and we do not sell your data.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Full access is offered as an auto-renewable weekly subscription with a free trial for new subscribers. Payments, renewals and cancellations are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We do not receive or store your card or billing information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

5. How long we keep data

  • Analytics event data is kept according to the Google Analytics retention setting for the App, up to 14 months, and then deleted.
  • Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or the App.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn tracking off at any time, as described in section 3.
  • Delete the App to remove all data stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete data about you. Because we do not know who you are, contact us and we will help identify and delete analytics data for your installation.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes.
